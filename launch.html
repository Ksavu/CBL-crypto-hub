    How a launch works

    You sign once

    One payment from your wallet funds the launch. Nothing else needs your signature.

    Token is minted

    A fresh issuer account mints your supply and gives up freeze powers.

    Pool goes live

    Your XRP and tokens form the XRP pool on the ledger's built-in AMM. Tradeable instantly.

    Everything is locked

    The issuer and the pool wallet are blackholed. No more minting, and liquidity can never be withdrawn.

    Questions

    Does CBL hold my funds or keys?

    No. The two launch accounts are created in your browser and signed there. CBL's server only reads the XRP Ledger to verify and list your token.

    What does a launch cost?

    Your starting liquidity, the CBL launch fee, and about 2.7 XRP of XRP Ledger reserves that stay locked in the launch accounts.

    Can I add liquidity or change anything later?

    Anyone can add liquidity to the pool later, but the launch liquidity is locked forever. The supply, the pool fee and the issuer settings can't be changed by anyone, including you.

    What if my browser closes during the launch?

    Progress is saved in this browser. Reopen this page and press Resume. If the pool wasn't created yet, you can also cancel and get the unused XRP back.

    Is a launched token "safe"?

    Locked supply and liquidity remove the most common rug pulls, but a token can still lose value. Always do your own research.
